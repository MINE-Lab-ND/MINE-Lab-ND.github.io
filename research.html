What we work on

Research.

The MINE Lab advances cutting-edge AI across trustworthy foundation models, AI for science, and graph-based learning — bridging theoretical innovation with tangible impact.

01

Socially Impactful and Trustworthy Foundation Models

We pursue a principled understanding and development of foundation models, emphasizing state-of-the-art performance alongside societal responsibility. A key thread is the trustworthy interpretation of generative models. We also study strategies that improve alignment and general intelligence, harmonizing model objectives with human values — ultimately harnessing foundation models to drive meaningful progress across critical downstream domains.

02

AI for Science

At the intersection of AI and the sciences, we apply generative and predictive models to accelerate progress in chemistry (molecular design, reaction prediction, material discovery) and, more recently, in the social sciences. With large-scale data and model-based simulation, we uncover patterns in individual behavior, social norms, and policy impact.

03

Graph-Based Learning for Real-World Problem Solving

Many real-world problems — social networks, recommendation systems, molecular structures — live naturally as graphs. We design graph learning methods for tasks spanning drug discovery, recommendation, and knowledge graph construction, using graph structure to surface patterns and improve decision-making across diverse domains.
