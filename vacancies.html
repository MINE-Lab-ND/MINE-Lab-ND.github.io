Join Our Team

Opportunities to work with us at the MINE Lab

The MINE Lab at the University of Notre Dame has several openings for talented and motivated researchers who are passionate about advancing AI through creative research and practical applications.

We are seeking individuals who are excited to work on cutting-edge topics in machine learning, knowledge engineering, and AI applications in science. Our lab values innovation, collaboration, and a commitment to developing AI solutions that are both technically excellent and socially beneficial.

PhD Positions

We are looking for PhD students with strong backgrounds in computer science, mathematics, or related fields to join our team. Successful candidates will work on research projects in areas such as:

  • Foundation models and language understanding
  • Trustworthy and responsible AI
  • Graph-based machine learning
  • AI for scientific discovery (Chemistry, Biology, Physics)
  • Knowledge representation and reasoning

Requirements

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Mathematics, Statistics or related field
  • Strong programming skills and experience with machine learning frameworks
  • Excellent academic record and research potential
  • Good communication skills in English

Application Process

Applications for PhD positions should be submitted through the Notre Dame Graduate School application system. Please mention your interest in the MINE Lab in your statement of purpose.

Postdoctoral Researchers

With the support of Lucy Family Institute for Data & Society, the Foundation Models and Applications Lab (FMAL) is launched! It is co-directed by Prof. Xiangliang Zhang and Prof. Meng Jiang. There is an opening position for a postdoctoral researcher. The research topic is Foundation Models and Applications, emphasizing interdisciplinary collaborations. Contact us if you are interested!

Requirements

  • PhD in Computer Science, Machine Learning, AI or related field
  • Strong publication record in top-tier conferences or journals
  • Experience with deep learning frameworks and large-scale experiments
  • Excellent research skills and independence

Application Process

To apply for a postdoctoral position, please send your curriculum vitae and list of publications to xzhang33@nd.edu with the subject line "Postdoc Application - FMAL".

Visiting Students and Researchers

Why Notre Dame?

The University of Notre Dame offers an exceptional environment for advanced research and learning

Academic Excellence & Prestige

Notre Dame consistently ranks among the nation's elite universities, currently ranked 18th in the U.S. News & World Report National University Rankings. The university boasts exceptional rankings in specific programs, including the 4th best undergraduate business program (Mendoza College of Business) and 22nd best engineering program nationwide. With a selective acceptance rate and a strong faculty-to-student ratio of 1:10.9, Notre Dame delivers world-class education across its comprehensive graduate programs in Engineering, Science, Arts and Letters, and the Keough School of Global Affairs.

Research Infrastructure & Funding

With state-of-the-art facilities and substantial research funding, Notre Dame has seen research funding increase from $87 million to over $141.5 million in the past decade. The university's operating budget exceeds $1.5 billion, with an endowment valued at nearly $10 billion. Notre Dame encourages interdisciplinary collaboration across its many research centers and institutes, including the Lucy Family Institute for Data & Society, providing extensive resources for cutting-edge research.
